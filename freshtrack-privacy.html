FreshTrack Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

The short version

FreshTrack stores your business data in your own iCloud account (CloudKit private database) and on your devices. We — Code Aeternum — cannot see, read or collect your customers, orders, invoices or payments. There is no account, no analytics, no tracking, no advertising and no third-party data sharing.

Where your data lives

  • iCloud (CloudKit private database). Clients, products, orders, invoices, payments, quotes, transporters and company information sync between your iPhone, iPad and Mac through your personal iCloud container (iCloud.com.ascnex.freshtrack). Only devices signed in with your Apple ID can read it. Apple hosts this data under its own privacy policy.
  • On your devices. Documents render to PDF locally. Purchase orders you import are processed on-device with Apple's Vision framework — the text and images never leave your device except into your own iCloud.
  • Backups and exports. JSON backups and PDF exports are created only when you choose to make them, and are saved where you decide.

What the app accesses

  • Face ID / Touch ID — optional app lock. Biometric data is handled entirely by iOS (LocalAuthentication); the app only receives a yes/no result.
  • Files and photos — only items you explicitly pick, for importing POs or your company logo.
  • Mail — the app can pre-fill a confirmation email draft; you review and send it yourself.
  • Notifications — optional reminders (e.g. invoices coming due), scheduled on your device.

Data we do not collect

No analytics SDKs, no crash-reporting services, no advertising identifiers, no location, no contacts, no usage metrics. The app makes no network requests to Code Aeternum servers — the only network traffic is between your devices and Apple's iCloud.

Deleting your data

Delete records inside the app or uninstall it. To remove the synced copy, delete the app's data in Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage, or contact us for help.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address.

Política de Privacidad de FreshTrack

Última actualización: 20 de octubre de 2026

FreshTrack guarda los datos de tu negocio en tu propia cuenta de iCloud (base privada de CloudKit) y en tus dispositivos. Nosotros — Code Aeternum — no podemos ver ni recolectar tus clientes, órdenes, facturas ni pagos. No hay cuentas, analítica, rastreo ni publicidad, y ningún dato se comparte con terceros.

Los POs que importas se procesan en el dispositivo con Vision de Apple; los PDFs se generan localmente; Face ID / Touch ID solo devuelve sí/no a la app. La app no hace peticiones a servidores de Code Aeternum — el único tráfico es entre tus dispositivos y el iCloud de Apple.
