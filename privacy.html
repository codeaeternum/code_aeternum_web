Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 16, 2026

1. Introduction

Code Aeternum respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your data when you use our software and website.

2. Data Collection in Extensions

Our browser extensions (including TG Media Grabber Pro) are designed with privacy first. We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal browsing data, messages, or media content to our servers.

All processing of media files happens locally on your device within the browser environment.

3. Donations

Our extensions are free to use. If you choose to support development via donation platforms (such as Ko-fi), those transactions are processed entirely by the third-party platform. Code Aeternum does not receive or store your payment details.

4. Feedback Data

If you submit feedback through our extension, the message content and feedback type are sent to a Google Form. The form does not ask for your name or email unless you voluntarily include them in the message. Google may process technical request data according to its own privacy policy.

5. Website and Infrastructure

Our website does not embed advertising pixels or third-party analytics scripts. It is hosted by GitHub Pages and delivered through Cloudflare. These providers may process technical request information such as IP address, browser type, requested page, and timestamps to deliver, secure, and operate the site.

Cloudflare provides us with aggregate traffic metrics. We use those totals to understand site reliability and general usage, not to identify or profile individual visitors. The site also loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive a technical request from your browser.

6. Product-Specific Policies

Some products have a separate privacy notice describing their exact behavior. For example, Vunari's privacy notice is available on its support site.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at soporte@codeaeternum.com.
