FreshTrack

From the customer's purchase order to the final payment — without losing a single fact along the way.

Contact Support Privacy Policy

Built for the real commercial flow

FreshTrack keeps every stage of a sale as its own document, so nothing gets lost or overwritten:

  • Purchase order — exactly what the customer asked for (import PDF POs with on-device text recognition).
  • Commercial confirmation — what you actually committed, with per-line quantities and prices.
  • Delivery document — the remisión your driver carries, generated from confirmed quantities.
  • Final review — what was accepted after returned paperwork: shortages, damage, substitutions, price changes.
  • Invoice & collections — credits, deductions, partial payments, bounced checks and full payment history.

Features

  • Orders, amendments, delivery notes, invoices, credit notes and payment application
  • Configurable document numbering — global and per-customer series
  • Clients, products, transporters and price lists per customer
  • Professional PDFs in English and Spanish, ready to print or share
  • Dashboard with today's deliveries and invoices coming due
  • iCloud sync between iPhone, iPad and Mac — your data stays in your account
  • Face ID / Touch ID lock, JSON backups and restore

Download for Mac

FreshTrack for macOS is signed with an Apple Developer ID and notarized — install it straight from the DMG, no App Store needed. It syncs with the iPhone and iPad app over your iCloud account.

Download FreshTrack 1.0 for Mac

Requires macOS 14 or later. Open the DMG and drag FreshTrack to Applications. The iOS app is distributed via the App Store.
